# Administrative

This folder holds supervision records, project progress tracking and the formal documents
that require approval or sign-off. It is divided into four areas.

## Supervision/

Meeting logs (date, attendees, items discussed, decisions, action items), the
supervision or attendance form, and recorded feedback.

## Progress/

The work plan (Gantt chart, timeline or milestone schedule) and the running progress log
used to track the project against its planned schedule.

## Approvals/

The ethical clearance form (editable version and signed PDF) and the title clearance form.

## Declarations/

The declaration of originality, the plagiarism or similarity report, and the participant
information sheet with consent form.

## Maintenance

Meetings should be logged on the same day they occur. The progress log should be updated
regularly so that it reflects the current status of the work. Both the editable and
signed copies of each approval should be retained. Where the research involves
participants, consent forms must be completed before any data is collected.
