# sources.md — the honesty ledger
_Every claim/definition/statistic/method the model hands you: is it checked against a
primary source? Full reference once verified. Unverified = flagged and UNUSED._
_This keeps you honest (§7) AND seeds your literature review._

## Status key
[VERIFIED] I opened the primary source and confirmed it.
[TO-VERIFY] Plausible, model-supplied or search-found, NOT yet personally confirmed.
[FLAG] Needs a specific check noted below before it can be used.

## Rule the supervisor's guide sets (recency)
Lecturer wants post-2023 EVIDENCE, because gaps found in old papers may be closed. Old
FOUNDATIONAL papers (SERVQUAL, AHP, DSR, VADER) are origin citations — ask supervisor
whether to keep them alongside a recent application, or cite only the recent one.
